Cozy Town Free — 16x16 top-down town tileset by Spaceyyy

A free slice of Cozy Town, big enough to make a small game: one full street, three shops
from the outside, the cafe from the inside and four characters. Same 16 x 16 grid, palette
and warm 1 px outline as Cozy Town Complete and Cozy Farm.

Free for personal and non-commercial projects, including game jams. Commercial use, all 522 tiles
(plus 1046 sprites) and future updates: Cozy Town Complete
https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-town

WHAT'S INSIDE
  293 unique tiles, 116 sprites and 432 character animation frames
  (about 26% of Cozy Town Complete). Every PNG comes in 1x, 2x and 4x.

  Street ............ 221 ground tiles (185 unique): asphalt and cobblestone streets, center
                      lines, crosswalks, curb ramps, manholes and drains, sidewalks, plaza paving,
                      brick, grass and tree pits, with the three 47-piece blob autotiles and the
                      16-piece planter. tiles/town-ground.json names every tile and its autotile mask.
  Buildings ......... 6 sprites: cafe, bakery and flower shop, each by day and by night.
  Cafe interior ..... 108 floor, wall and room-frame tiles, 51 cafe furniture pieces (counter,
                      pastry cases, espresso machine, tables and chairs, booths, armchairs, sofa,
                      rugs, menu boards) and 13 shared pieces (plants, lamps, clock, pictures,
                      coat rack, a sleeping cat). tiles/interior.json names them all.
  Street props ...... 40 sprites: terrace tables and chairs, patio umbrellas, sidewalk signs,
                      hanging signs, an awning, lampposts on and off, benches, bus stop, traffic
                      lights, road signs (icons only), bike rack and bicycles, mailbox, trash cans,
                      hydrant, bollard, planters, hedge and crates. sprites/props.json lists them.
  Nature ............ 6 sprites: street trees (green, apple, autumn, pine), bush, wildflowers.
  Characters ........ 4: players 1 and 2 with 8 animations in 4 directions (idle, walk, run, carry,
                      sit, wave, eat, water), and the barista and the baker with idle and walk in
                      4 directions. 16 x 24 frames; see characters/README-characters.txt.

  examples/01-main-street.png shows how the pieces fit together (made only with what is in
  this pack; examples/1x/ has the same scene at 1x).

FOLDERS
  tiles/        ground and interior tile sheets, with their .json
  sprites/      one PNG per object: buildings/, interiors/, props/, nature/
  characters/   one sheet per character, characters.json and README-characters.txt
  examples/     the sample scene

Works in any engine that reads PNG sheets on a 16 px grid: Godot, Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker,
Tiled, Defold, LOVE, Phaser...
